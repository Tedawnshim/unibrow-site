This policy explains what information Unibrow Studio collects when you visit unibrow.studio or contact us, and how we use it. Unibrow Studio is the trading name of Rough Cut Productions Pvt. Ltd., based in Imphal, Manipur, India.

## What we collect

- **When you fill in our contact form:** your name, phone number, email, business name, the service you're interested in, a rough budget, your timeline, how you found us, and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to prepare a proposal if you ask for one.
- **When you visit the site:** we use Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and how they found us. It records things like the pages you view, the device and browser you use, and your approximate location (city level). It does not tell us who you are.
- **When you message, call or email us:** what you choose to share, in the app you use (WhatsApp, phone or email).

## How the form works

The contact form is handled by Netlify, the company that hosts our website. Your message is stored in our Netlify account and sent to our email inbox, which is run by Zoho Mail.

## Videos

Some pages show films hosted on YouTube. The YouTube player loads only when you press play, and uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Once you press play, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

## Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell repeat visits apart. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them. [If we add the Meta Pixel or other advertising tools, we will add a consent banner and update this section first.]

## Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for anyone else's marketing. We use these services to run the site and reply to you: Netlify (hosting and forms), Google Analytics (visit statistics), YouTube (video), and Zoho Mail (email).

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and to work with you, and delete them when they are no longer needed. [Agree a period, e.g. 24 months after the last contact.]

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Write to [contact@unibrow.studio](mailto:contact@unibrow.studio).

## Children

This site is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
