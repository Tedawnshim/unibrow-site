We want unibrow.studio to work for everyone, whatever device or ability they bring. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we have done

- **Contrast:** our text and background colours meet the AA contrast ratio. Our lighter blue is used only for large handwritten notes and decorative numbers.
- **Structure:** each page has one main heading, and headings follow in order, so screen readers can move through the page.
- **Images:** images that carry meaning have descriptions. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
- **Keyboard:** every link, button and form field can be reached and used with a keyboard, and shows a clear outline when focused.
- **Zoom and phones:** the site works on small screens and when zoomed to 200%. Pinch-to-zoom is never blocked.
- **Motion:** films never play by themselves. We respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.

## What we are still working on

- Some of our films do not yet have captions. We are adding them, starting with our newest work.
- Films are hosted on YouTube, whose player is outside our control.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and what went wrong. We reply within 5 working days.
