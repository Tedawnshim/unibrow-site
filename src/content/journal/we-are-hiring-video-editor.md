## A STUDIO THAT TAKES WORK SERIOUSLY

We are a creative studio and media house that believes in authentic, human-centric storytelling. Our motto is "connect. collaborate. create." We are on a mission to build an ethical, inclusive, and professional media house that provides quality services and nurtures talent.

Our work is two-fold — we partner with clients from local grassroots enterprises to large-scale national foundations, and we produce our own original content including our flagship documentary-style shows and podcasts that celebrate resilience and grace in everyday life.

## NOT JUST A CUTTER. A STORYTELLER.

We need someone who understands the silence in a documentary interview just as well as they understand the fast-paced rhythm required for an Instagram Reel. You will be the bridge between our high-end cinematic footage and the audience's attention span — working closely with the Creative Director and Social Media Manager.

### Long-Form Narrative

Edit full-length episodes of our docu-series (Everyday Ordinary), typically 30 minutes to 1 hour. You need the patience to find the emotional payoff within hours of raw footage.

### Repurposing with Intent

Slice long-form content into compelling Shorts and Reels. Take a 10-minute deep conversation and turn it into a 60-second hook without losing the soul of the story.

### Audio & Colour

Perform basic sound mixing and colour correction to ensure the atmosphere and authenticity of the footage is preserved across all deliverables.

### Client Content

Work with our production team to create, schedule, and publish client content. Manage and optimize basic paid social campaigns and provide clear monthly analytics reports.

## OUR IDEAL CANDIDATE

**The Hybrid Editor —** 2–4 years of proven experience. Comfortable in the timeline of a long-form documentary, but you also live on social media and know what makes a Reel perform.

**Tool Agnostic but Proficient —** DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or Final Cut — we don't mind, as long as you are fast, organised, and deliver quality.

**Motion Graphics Skills —** Bonus points if you can handle motion graphics using Fusion, After Effects, or similar tools.

**A Student of Attention —** You edit for psychology, not just aesthetics. You know why a cut works. You understand that the first 5 seconds on YouTube matter, and the first 3 on Instagram.

**Collaborative Spirit —** You leave your ego at the door. You take feedback and make the work better. You believe in lifting others up.

**Organised Pro —** Impeccable file structure and version control. Any team member can open your project and understand exactly what is going on.

**Mission-Driven —** You resonate with our values: Ethical, Professional, Collaborative, and Empathetic.
