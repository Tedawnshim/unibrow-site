## A STUDIO THAT TAKES WORK SERIOUSLY

We are a creative studio and media house that believes in authentic, human-centric storytelling. Our motto is "connect. collaborate. create." We are on a mission to build an ethical, inclusive, and professional media house that provides quality services and nurtures talent.

Our work is two-fold, we partner with clients from local grassroots enterprises to large-scale national foundations, and we produce our own original content including our flagship documentary-style shows and podcasts that celebrate resilience and grace in everyday life.

## ARCHITECT OF VOICE & GROWTH

We seek a Social Media Manager to build and lead our brand's presence. This foundational role demands a strategic storyteller who will be the authentic voice of Unibrow Studio and its original productions.

Simultaneously, you will drive our clients' success by managing their accounts, owning client relations, and delivering measurable results.

### Brand Strategy

Develop and execute the social media strategy to build the Unibrow Studio brand and grow the audience for our original content.

### Content Creation

Coordinate with our internal creative team to produce a pipeline of compelling, on-brand content, graphics, short video clips, carousels, stories.

### Client Management

Serve as the main point of contact for a portfolio of clients. Develop unique social media strategies and content calendars tailored to each client's voice and goals.

### Analytics & Reporting

Set up, manage, and optimise basic paid social campaigns. Provide clear, concise monthly analytics reports to clients, explaining what the numbers mean and proposing next steps.

### Community

Engage with our community across all platforms, fostering meaningful conversations and building relationships that go beyond the feed.

### Copywriting

Write authentic, engaging, and thoughtful copy that reflects our "connect. collaborate. create." ethos across studio and client accounts.

> We're not looking for someone to post. We're looking for someone to build.

## OUR IDEAL CANDIDATE

2–3 years of proven experience as a Social Media Manager, preferably in a creative agency or media environment where you handled multiple accounts.

You have a great eye for what makes compelling content. You can manage the content pipeline with our creative team and are skilled at creating your own professional graphics (Canva is your second language).

A strong copywriter who can switch tone of voice from our thoughtful studio brand to a diverse range of client needs, from corporate to grassroots.

A master of time management and client communication. You can juggle multiple accounts, content calendars, and deadlines without breaking a sweat.

You understand the numbers. You can read social media analytics and translate them into actionable insights for both our team and our clients.

You're not just looking for another job. You believe in our mission and share our core values: Ethical, Professional, Collaborative, and Empathetic.
